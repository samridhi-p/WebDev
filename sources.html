Forecast and Alert Workflow

  1. Collect weather observations.
  2. Validate and process the collected data.
  3. Analyse temperature and heat-index conditions.
  4. Generate the heatwave forecast.
  5. Issue alerts to stakeholders and citizens.

Data Sources

  • AWS sensors
  • IMD database
  • Satellite feeds
  • Weather forecast models

Stakeholders and Available Actions

  • Citizens
    • View alerts
    • Subscribe to advisories
    • View safety recommendations
  • Meteorologists
    • Monitor weather observations
    • Review forecasts
    • Update alert bulletins
  • Disaster Management Authorities
    • Monitor severe alerts
    • Coordinate emergency response
    • Issue public safety instructions
  • Healthcare Departments
    • Monitor heat-related health risks
    • Prepare healthcare facilities
    • Issue health advisories
