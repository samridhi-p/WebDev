Objective

The objective of the Heatwave Intelligence System is to monitor extreme temperature conditions and provide timely warnings to communities.

The system helps reduce heat-related risks through early warning and public awareness.

Data Sources

The portal combines data from weather stations, satellite observations and meteorological databases.

  • Automatic Weather Stations
  • IMD meteorological databases
  • Satellite observations
  • Forecast models

Stakeholders

The system supports citizens, meteorologists, health departments and disaster-management authorities.

"Early warnings save lives. Accurate heatwave information allows communities and authorities to act before extreme heat becomes a major emergency."

— Chief Meteorologist / Disaster Management Officer
